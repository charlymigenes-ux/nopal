# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NOPAL's community — issues, pull requests, discussions, and the collaboration program — to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or the hardware they own.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a technical decision.
- Assume good faith. Most bug reports, "obvious" questions, and imperfect first pull requests come from people trying to help.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Publishing others' private information (including data submitted through the collaboration program) without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all NOPAL spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions), the collaboration program, and any official community channels linked from the README.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private report via GitHub, or by contacting the maintainer directly through the contact methods listed on the [NOPAL site](https://charlymigenes-ux.github.io/nopal/). Reports will be reviewed and may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from project spaces, at the maintainer's discretion.
